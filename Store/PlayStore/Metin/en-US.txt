TITLE (12/30)
Neon Pinball

SHORT DESCRIPTION (72/80)
100 levels, 10 worlds, endless mode. Build combos, beat records. No ads.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Neon Pinball brings the feel of the arcade hall back on a table that glows. Launch the ball, keep it alive with the flippers, and hit every bumper and target.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the left or right half of the screen to work the flippers.
• Drag or tap the plunger on the right to launch the ball.
• Hit different targets within 3 seconds to build a combo. The higher the combo level, the more each hit is worth.
• Every two completed sets raise your multiplier from 1X up to 4X.

FEATURES
• 100 handcrafted levels across 10 worlds
• 3 gems on every level: finish it, reach the score goal, complete the bonus objective
• Endless mode with Classic and Modern table styles and separate records
• Smooth, realistic physics and precise controls
• Glowing neon visuals with haptic and sound feedback
• 7 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic
• No ads, no account, no internet needed
