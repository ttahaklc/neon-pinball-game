Neon Pinball – Support

Need help or want to send feedback? Write to YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description.

How do I launch the ball?

Drag the plunger on the right downward and release, or simply tap it for a standard launch. In Settings you will find a full How to Play guide.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes the save.
