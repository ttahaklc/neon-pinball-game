Summary

Neon Pinball does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline and has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics.

Your progress (levels completed, gems, records) and settings (language, sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes this data.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, identifiers or usage data.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs and does not send information to any third party.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
